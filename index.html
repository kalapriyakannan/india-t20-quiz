Summary of the final, India vs South Africa, Kensington Oval, Bridgetown, 29 June 2024 (written from public match reports).

Overview. India beat South Africa by 7 runs to win their second T20 World Cup and their first ICC trophy since 2013. It was South Africa’s first-ever men’s World Cup final. India won the toss and batted, finishing on 176/7. South Africa replied with 169/8. Virat Kohli was Player of the Match and Jasprit Bumrah was Player of the Tournament. Kohli announced his retirement from T20 internationals afterwards.
India bat. Keshav Maharaj struck twice in the second over, removing Rohit Sharma (caught by Klaasen) and Rishabh Pant (caught by de Kock) within three balls. Kohli rebuilt the innings with 76 off 59 balls, supported by a quick 47 from Axar Patel, who was run out. Shivam Dube added 27 and Hardik Pandya finished unbeaten. Anrich Nortje took two late wickets, including Dube and Jadeja.
South Africa chase. Bumrah bowled Reeza Hendricks in the second over and Arshdeep Singh got captain Markram caught behind soon after. Quinton de Kock and Tristan Stubbs rebuilt, but Axar bowled Stubbs and Arshdeep removed de Kock. Heinrich Klaasen then launched a counter-attack, hitting 52 off 27 balls with five sixes, including a 24-run over off Axar.
The turning point. Klaasen was caught behind off Pandya, leaving South Africa needing 26 from 23 balls. Bumrah then bowled Marco Jansen with a ball through the gate, leaving 21 needed from 14. David Miller was later caught on the boundary by Suryakumar Yadav off Pandya, and Rabada fell to Pandya as well, again caught by Suryakumar.
